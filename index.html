About

Reading galaxies through their magnetism

I am a Ph.D. candidate at the National Institute of Science Education and Research (NISER), India, working under the supervision of Dr. Tuhin Ghosh. My research focuses on modeling large-scale magnetic fields in nearby spiral galaxies using multi-frequency radio polarization data.

Alongside this, I contribute to the instrumental simulation of the Wide Area Linear Optical Polarimeter (WALOP) for the PASIPHAE survey, and I build PSF-based photometric pipelines for accurate polarization estimation.

Research interests
  • Galactic magnetic fields from radio observations
  • Astrostatistics and Bayesian modeling
  • Machine learning applications in astrophysics
  • Instrumental simulations and PSF photometry
  • Astronomical image processing
